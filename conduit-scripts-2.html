Conduit — Ad Campaign Scripts

The Four Scripts

Script 1

Yesterday I spent the whole day jackhammering out a slab of concrete, and then I drove across town to finish another job. When I got home, the last thing I wanted to do was build a website. I’d been telling myself I needed one for years, and I’d bet you have too.
Conduit is the software I use to run my business. I send my bids from it, I schedule my jobs in it, and I send my invoices from it. Now Conduit builds you a website for a $200 one-time fee. You pick a template, answer a few questions, and the site plugs directly into Conduit. That means customers can pay your invoices, request estimates, and see a gallery of your best jobs right from your website, and every one of those lands in the same place you run the rest of your business.
A lot of guys pay an agency every month to keep their site up, and the day they stop paying, the site goes away. With Conduit, you pay for the site once. Conduit itself is thirty-five bucks a month, and that covers your bids, your scheduling, and your invoicing too.
If you’ve been putting your website off as long as I did, start a free trial and we’ll build it for you.

Script 2

Check this out. I’m in a house we’re wiring up right now, and I’m going to write a bid while you watch this ad.
“[Scope for the wall]”
And it’s done. Conduit took what I just said and wrote it up as a bid with every line item on it, and I can send that to the customer before I’m back in my truck. I used to write these late at night at my kitchen table. Now I write them on my phone, and I can share the bid with the customer while I’m still there for their estimate.
As a bonus, if you have an iPhone, which I don’t, you can walk the room and Conduit uses LiDAR to draw the floor plan for you.
Start a free trial and send your next bid from the job site.

Script 3

As a contractor, getting paid is supposed to be the easiest part of the job, and for a lot of us it’s the hardest. I’ve chased people down for money on work I already finished, and I hate having that conversation because it’s always awkward. Now with Conduit, I don’t even have to think about it. It’s like magic. I just get paid.
Well, it’s not really magic. Here’s what Conduit is doing while Avery’s working. If you have a maintenance contract or a job that bills every month, your customer saves their card or bank account once, and Conduit charges them on schedule. You set a start date and an end date, so a five-month remodel bills for five months and then stops. When you send the invoice, Conduit works out the local sales tax from your customer’s address. The invoice also shows up in QuickBooks or Xero on its own, so you never have to create it twice.
I do the work, and the money comes in. Start a free trial and set up your first autopay today.

Script 4

I just found out I’m going to be a dad, which is crazy and exciting, AND as an electrician and a small business owner, I know my time is about to get even more precious. Wiring up a new build isn’t going to get any faster, but on the admin side— anywhere I can cut hours is time I get to spend with my kid.
I think a lot of software companies assume we have somebody else doing the admin, and most of us don’t. What I like about building Conduit is that we’re building it for contractors and small business owners who do it all themselves. A lot of them are like me. They’ve got their first kid on the way, they’re doing five site visits a week, and they’re handling every bit of the paperwork on their own. We’re building Conduit in a way so that all that extra admin work, like writing bids, collecting payments, and keeping projects on track, just happens in the background.
If you’re running your business and your life at the same time, start a free trial and get some of those hours back.
