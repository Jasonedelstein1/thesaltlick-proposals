The Three Scripts

Script 1 — Why We Switched

Deanna
Earlier this year, we made one of the biggest changes we've ever made at Vibrant Health.
After 15 years of practicing in the traditional insurance model, we made the decision to transition to a direct primary care practice and intentionally limit our patient panels.
Why?
Because we wanted to practice medicine differently.
We were tired of rushed appointments, long waits to get in, providers burning out, and patients feeling like there was never enough time to really address what was going on.
So, we changed it.
At Vibrant Health, our patients pay a monthly or annual membership fee that covers their care with us. That includes comprehensive primary care, gynecology, hormone care and weight management. And all of our providers also practice functional medicine, so when we see you for anything we are looking at the whole picture.
But what really makes this model different is time and access.
Time to listen.
Time to look deeper.
Time to actually know you.
If your provider wants to see you again in two weeks, there's no additional visit charge. If something comes up between appointments, you can reach us. And when you need to be seen, we're usually able to get you in quickly.
To make that possible, each of our providers cares for a limited number of patients. Some of our provider panels are already full, while our remaining panels in Lone Tree and Eagle continue to grow.
We're incredibly grateful for the patients who have chosen to experience healthcare differently with us.
If you've been looking for healthcare that feels more personal, more comprehensive, and a little more like what medicine was always supposed to be, we'd love to meet you.

Script 2 — Hormones

Deanna
If you've been dealing with hot flashes, night sweats, fatigue, brain fog, insomnia, or low libido, and you've been told your labs look normal, I want you to know something. While these symptoms are really common in women in their 40s to 50s, we shouldn't accept them as normal.
We see this all the time. Women come to us with symptoms that are genuinely disrupting their lives, and they keep hearing that everything looks fine. It is so frustrating to know something is wrong and be told that everything is normal.
I've been helping patients work through hormone imbalance for more than twenty years, and I built this practice around the idea that people deserve to be listened to and validated. You know your body. If it feels like something is wrong, something is wrong. And we will keep digging until we figure it out. We start with comprehensive labs, as there are often many factors, even when hormone imbalance is the major one.
Every provider in our office is a functional medicine provider. That means the care you'd normally be referred to a specialist for, like hormone therapy or weight management, is included in your membership, right alongside your primary care and your gynecology. You'll see a provider who knows you by name, and you'll never get a separate bill from us.
We have offices in Lone Tree and in Eagle, and are so grateful to the patients who have trusted us with their health. If you are tired of not being listened to and want to experience a different type of healthcare, fill out the contact form below with your information, and we'll be in touch. You've waited long enough.

Script 3 — Family Membership

Erin
Here's something that happens at our office all the time.
A woman calls us because she's struggling with her hormones, her energy, her sleep, or maybe she just doesn't feel like herself anymore.
And a month later… her husband is our patient too.
I'm Erin, and I take a lot of those first calls. What usually starts as a conversation about her turns into, "Wait, you see men too?"
We do.
And pretty soon we're talking about his testosterone, his weight, or maybe the fact that he hasn't had a primary care provider who really knows him in years.
That's one of the things I love about Vibrant Health. We're not just a hormone clinic or a functional medicine office. We can actually take care of your family.
Primary care, gynecology, hormones, weight management, urgent care and functional medicine can all happen in one place, with providers who have the time to communicate with each other and really get to know you.
Because we're a direct primary care practice, our providers care for a fraction of the number of patients they would in a traditional practice. That means longer appointments, same-day availability when you need us, and access to your healthcare team between visits.
We also offer discounted family memberships, making it easier for couples and families to receive this kind of care together.
We intentionally limit the number of patients each provider cares for so we can protect the time and access that make this model different. Some of our provider panels are already full, and our remaining panels continue to grow.
If you'd like to learn more, fill out your information below. I'll give you a call, you can tell me what you're looking for, and I'll help you figure out which of our providers would be the best fit.
And don't be surprised if the rest of your family ends up joining you.
